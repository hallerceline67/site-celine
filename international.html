My project — Un Bol à l’École

“Un Bol à l’École” is a school-based breakfast program designed to support vulnerable children through a daily educational ritual. It connects nutrition, emotional security and learning engagement, with a strong focus on equity and inclusion.

Why it matters

  • Creates a safe, predictable start of the day
  • Improves attention and classroom climate
  • Strengthens relationships with families
  • Reduces inequality in access to learning
